# Week 5: power, effect size and minimum detectable effect

Pooled within-country standard deviation, 2015 onward: 1019.4 points.
Observations per group available: 60.
Minimum detectable effect at alpha 0.05 and power 0.80: 521.1 percentage points.
A ten per cent relative change on the regional mean of 680.2 is about 68.0 points, which is NOT detectable with the available n.

This is the calculation that lets a consultant tell a client their evaluation cannot see
the effect they care about, before the budget is spent rather than after. It is also the
calculation most terms of reference omit.

Caveats: annual observations are serially correlated so effective n is lower and the true
MDE is larger; the normal-approximation constants ignore that the variance is estimated.
Both make this MDE optimistic, so treat it as a floor.
